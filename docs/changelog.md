## 0.1.0

- First release. Banner with Accept all, Reject all and Manage preferences; preferences window with Necessary, Preferences, Analytics and Marketing and the cookies in each.
- Nothing optional runs before consent: tagged scripts and embeds wait, well-known trackers are held automatically, Google Consent Mode v2 signals are sent before any tag loads.
- Every choice is recorded with a consent ID the visitor can quote. Searchable in the admin.
- Cookie discovery: cookie names seen on the site that are not listed yet show up for one-click categorising.
- Layout, position, colours and all wording editable. Banner can be limited to EU, UK and Swiss visitors.
- Ask everyone again after changing the cookie setup.
